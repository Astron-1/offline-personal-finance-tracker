Personal Finance Tracker

Simple. Private. Powerful.

What is this?

A straightforward expense tracking tool that helps you understand where your money goes. Track your spending, visualize patterns, and make better financial decisions—all without complicated setup or subscription fees.

Key Features

Easy Transaction Entry

Add expenses in seconds with amount, category, date, and optional notes.

Visual Insights

See your spending patterns with charts showing categories and monthly trends.

100% Private

All data stays in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Export & Import

Backup your data as JSON files. Full control, no vendor lock-in.

How It Works

1

Add Your Expenses

Enter the amount, pick a category (Food, Transport, Housing, etc.), and add the date. That's it!

2

View Your Spending

Browse all transactions in an organized list. Edit or delete entries anytime.

3

Analyze with Charts

Switch to the Dashboard to see visual breakdowns by category and monthly spending trends.

4

Keep It Safe

Export your data anytime to create backups. Import it back if you switch browsers or devices.

Your Data, Your Control

This app runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server, no accounts are created, and no one but you can see your transactions. Everything is stored locally on your device using your browser's storage.
